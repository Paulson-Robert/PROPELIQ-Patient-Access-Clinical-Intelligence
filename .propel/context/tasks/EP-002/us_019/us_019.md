# User Story - US_019

## Story ID

- ID Format: US_019

## Story Title

- Preferred Slot Swap Engine

## Description

- As a **patient**, I want to indicate a preferred time slot that is currently unavailable so that the system automatically swaps my appointment when that slot becomes available, using first-come-first-served ordering.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01: Patient registers a preferred slot preference

- **Given**: The patient has a confirmed appointment
- **When**: The patient selects an unavailable slot as their preferred time during or after booking
- **Then**: The system creates a PreferredSlotQueue record linking the patient's appointment to the preferred slot with a "Waiting" status and RequestedAt timestamp

### AC-02: Auto-swap triggers when preferred slot becomes available

- **Given**: A patient has a preferred slot preference in "Waiting" status
- **When**: The preferred slot becomes available (via cancellation or rescheduling by another patient)
- **Then**: The system acquires a lock on the preferred slot, moves the patient's appointment to the preferred slot, releases the original slot, updates the queue status to "Swapped", and sends notification to the patient via email and SMS

### AC-03: First-come-first-served ordering resolves multiple preferences

- **Given**: Three patients (A, B, C) have preferred the same unavailable slot with RequestedAt timestamps of 10:00, 10:05, and 10:10 respectively
- **When**: The preferred slot becomes available
- **Then**: Patient A (earliest RequestedAt) receives the swap; Patients B and C remain in "Waiting" status

### AC-04: Staff is notified of completed swap

- **Given**: A preferred slot swap has been executed
- **When**: The swap completes
- **Then**: Relevant staff members receive a notification with the patient name, original slot, and new slot details

### AC-05: Failed swap retains original appointment

- **Given**: A patient has a preferred slot preference
- **When**: The preferred slot becomes available but the lock acquisition fails (slot taken by another booking)
- **Then**: The patient retains their original appointment, the queue entry remains in "Waiting" status, and no notification is sent

### AC-06: Calendar sync updates after swap

- **Given**: A patient has calendar sync configured and a swap is executed
- **When**: The swap completes
- **Then**: The external calendar event is updated to reflect the new appointment time via an async background job

## Edge Cases

- **Appointment cancelled while in queue**: If a patient cancels their appointment while they have a "Waiting" queue entry, the queue entry should be updated to "Expired"
- **Preferred slot repeatedly becomes available and unavailable**: Each availability event should re-evaluate the queue; rapid toggling should not cause duplicate swap attempts due to Redis lock protection
- **Patient notification failure**: If swap notification delivery fails, it follows the retry pattern (max 3 attempts with exponential backoff); the swap itself is not reversed
