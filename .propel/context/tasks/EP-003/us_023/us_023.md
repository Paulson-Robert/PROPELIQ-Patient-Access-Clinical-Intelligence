# User Story - US_023

## Story ID

- ID Format: US_023

## Story Title

- Staff Walk-in Booking

## Description

- As a **staff member**, I want to create walk-in appointment bookings for patients who arrive without a prior appointment, optionally creating a patient account, so that walk-in patients are tracked in the system and added to the same-day queue.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01: Staff searches for existing patient during walk-in

- **Given**: The staff member is on the walk-in booking page (SCR-018)
- **When**: The staff member enters a patient name, phone, or email in the search field
- **Then**: The system displays matching patient records; staff can select an existing patient

### AC-02: Staff creates new patient account for unregistered walk-in

- **Given**: The staff member searches for a walk-in patient and no existing record is found
- **When**: The staff member clicks "Create New Patient" and enters basic demographics (name, phone, email)
- **Then**: A new patient account is created with the Patient role and linked to the walk-in booking

### AC-03: Walk-in appointment is created with walk-in indicator

- **Given**: The staff member has selected or created a patient
- **When**: The staff member selects an available slot or adds the patient to the same-day queue and confirms the booking
- **Then**: An Appointment record is created with BookingType "WalkIn" and "Scheduled" status, and the appointment appears in the staff queue with a visual walk-in badge

### AC-04: Guest walk-in without account creation

- **Given**: The walk-in patient does not want to create an account
- **When**: The staff member chooses to skip account creation
- **Then**: The appointment is created with minimal demographic data (name only) as a guest walk-in record

### AC-05: Multiple patient matches are presented for selection

- **Given**: The staff member searches with partial information
- **When**: Multiple patient records match the search query
- **Then**: The system displays all matching records with name, phone, email, and last visit date for the staff member to select the correct patient

### AC-06: Walk-in added to queue when no slots available

- **Given**: No appointment slots are available for walk-in scheduling
- **When**: The staff member creates a walk-in booking
- **Then**: The patient is added to the same-day queue with an estimated wait time displayed

## Edge Cases

- **Duplicate patient creation**: If the staff member creates a new patient with an email that already exists, the system should display a "Patient with this email already exists" warning and offer to use the existing record
- **Walk-in outside business hours**: The system should allow walk-in bookings regardless of configured availability slots, creating queue entries even if no formal slots are defined
- **Staff member creates appointment for themselves**: The system should not restrict walk-in bookings based on the staff member's identity — any staff member can create a walk-in for any patient
