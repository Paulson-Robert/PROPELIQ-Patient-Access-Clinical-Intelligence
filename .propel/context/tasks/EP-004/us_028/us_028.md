# User Story - US_028

## Story ID

- ID Format: US_028

## Story Title

- Staff Operational Notifications

## Description

- As a **staff member**, I want to receive in-app toast notifications when a preferred slot swap is executed or when a notification delivery permanently fails, so that I am aware of operational events that require my attention.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01: Staff receives swap notification with details

- **Given**: A preferred slot swap has been executed for a patient
- **When**: The swap completes
- **Then**: Relevant staff members receive a toast notification (UXR-503) with the patient name, original slot (date/time), new slot (date/time), and a "View Appointment" action link

### AC-02: Staff receives delivery failure notification

- **Given**: A notification delivery has permanently failed after all retry attempts
- **When**: The final retry exhausts
- **Then**: Staff receives a toast notification with the patient name, notification type (e.g., "Appointment Reminder"), failure reason, and a "View Patient" action link

### AC-03: Toast notifications auto-dismiss after 5 seconds

- **Given**: A staff toast notification is displayed
- **When**: 5 seconds elapse without user interaction
- **Then**: The toast auto-dismisses with a fade-out animation; the notification is still accessible in a notification history panel

### AC-04: Toast notifications can be manually dismissed

- **Given**: A staff toast notification is displayed
- **When**: The staff member clicks the dismiss button (X) on the toast
- **Then**: The toast is immediately dismissed

### AC-05: Multiple notifications stack without overlap

- **Given**: Multiple operational events occur in rapid succession
- **When**: Multiple toast notifications are triggered
- **Then**: Toasts stack vertically (newest on top) with a maximum of 3 visible simultaneously; additional toasts queue and display as visible ones dismiss

## Edge Cases

- **Staff offline when notification triggers**: If the staff member's browser tab is inactive or they are logged out, notifications should be queued and displayed when they next access the platform
- **Notification history overflow**: The notification history panel should show the most recent 50 notifications with a "Load more" option
- **Toast during modal interaction**: If a modal dialog is open when a toast triggers, the toast should appear above the modal overlay without disrupting the modal interaction
