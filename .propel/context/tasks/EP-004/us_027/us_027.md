# User Story - US_027

## Story ID

- ID Format: US_027

## Story Title

- Automated Appointment Reminder Pipeline

## Description

- As a **patient**, I want to receive automated appointment reminders via both SMS and email channels with risk-based escalation for higher no-show risk tiers, so that I am reminded of my upcoming appointments through my preferred channels with appropriate urgency.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01: Standard reminders sent for low-risk appointments

- **Given**: A patient has an upcoming appointment classified as Low no-show risk
- **When**: The reminder trigger times are reached (48 hours and 2 hours before appointment)
- **Then**: The system sends reminder notifications via both SMS and email channels at each trigger time

### AC-02: Escalated reminders sent for medium and high-risk appointments

- **Given**: A patient has an upcoming appointment classified as Medium or High no-show risk
- **When**: The escalated reminder trigger times are reached (7 days, 48 hours, 24 hours, and 2 hours before appointment)
- **Then**: The system sends additional reminder notifications via both SMS and email at each trigger time

### AC-03: Reminders are delivered asynchronously via Hangfire

- **Given**: A reminder trigger time is reached
- **When**: The reminder is dispatched
- **Then**: The SMS and email deliveries are processed as separate Hangfire background jobs, not blocking any user-facing operations

### AC-04: Failed delivery retries with exponential backoff

- **Given**: An SMS or email delivery attempt fails
- **When**: The delivery is retried
- **Then**: The system retries with exponential backoff (max 3 attempts per channel) and updates the Notification record's RetryCount, LastAttemptAt, and Status after each attempt

### AC-05: Permanent failure notifies staff

- **Given**: Both channels (SMS and email) have failed after all retry attempts for a reminder
- **When**: The final retry fails
- **Then**: The system creates a staff notification with the patient name, appointment details, notification type, and failure reason

### AC-06: Cancelled appointment stops remaining reminders

- **Given**: A patient has scheduled reminders for an upcoming appointment
- **When**: The appointment is cancelled
- **Then**: All remaining scheduled reminder jobs for that appointment are cancelled and no further reminders are sent

## Edge Cases

- **Risk tier changes after reminders scheduled**: If the no-show risk tier is recalculated between reminder dispatches (e.g., new history data), already-sent reminders are not affected but future reminders use the updated tier
- **Duplicate reminder prevention**: If a Hangfire job is accidentally enqueued twice, the system should use idempotency keys to prevent sending duplicate reminders for the same appointment and trigger time
- **SMS/email provider outage**: If a provider is completely unreachable (not just a single delivery failure), the system should log a critical alert and not exhaust retry attempts immediately
