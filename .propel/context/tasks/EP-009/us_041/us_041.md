# User Story - US_041

## Story ID

- ID Format: US_041

## Story Title

- No-Show Risk Scoring Algorithm

## Description

- As a **developer**, I want a deterministic weighted scoring algorithm that calculates a no-show risk score for each appointment based on four input factors (historical no-show count, appointment lead time, time-of-day pattern, new-patient flag), so that appointments can be classified into risk tiers for targeted reminder escalation.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01: Risk score calculated on appointment creation

- **Given**: A patient books a new appointment
- **When**: The appointment record is created
- **Then**: The system evaluates the four risk factors and calculates a weighted risk score, storing the result in the Appointment's NoShowRiskScore field and the NoShowRiskFactor record

### AC-02: Historical no-show count contributes to score

- **Given**: A patient has a history of no-shows
- **When**: The risk score is calculated
- **Then**: The patient's HistoricalNoShowCount from the NoShowRiskFactor record is weighted and contributes proportionally to the overall score

### AC-03: Appointment lead time contributes to score

- **Given**: An appointment is booked with a specific lead time (days between booking and appointment date)
- **When**: The risk score is calculated
- **Then**: The lead time is inversely weighted (shorter lead time = higher risk) and contributes to the overall score

### AC-04: Time-of-day pattern contributes to score

- **Given**: The appointment is scheduled for a specific time of day
- **When**: The risk score is calculated
- **Then**: The time-of-day pattern is factored based on historical no-show patterns for that time slot (e.g., early morning and late afternoon may have higher no-show rates)

### AC-05: New-patient flag increases risk baseline

- **Given**: A patient is booking for the first time (IsNewPatient = true)
- **When**: The risk score is calculated
- **Then**: The new-patient flag contributes a baseline risk increase to the overall score, reflecting the higher statistical no-show rate for new patients

### AC-06: Algorithm uses only available factors

- **Given**: A patient has incomplete risk factor data (e.g., new patient with no history)
- **When**: The risk score is calculated
- **Then**: The algorithm uses only the available factors, logs "Incomplete risk assessment" with the missing factors, and still produces a valid score

## Edge Cases

- **Patient with zero history**: A brand-new patient should receive a risk score based solely on new-patient flag, lead time, and time-of-day — producing a moderate-to-high baseline risk
- **Rescheduled appointment**: When an appointment is rescheduled, the risk score should be recalculated with the new lead time and time-of-day values
- **Same-day walk-in**: Walk-in appointments with zero lead time should be scored but the lead time factor should not disproportionately inflate the risk (the patient is already present)
