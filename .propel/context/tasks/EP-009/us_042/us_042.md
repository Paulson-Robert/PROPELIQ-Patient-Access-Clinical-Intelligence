# User Story - US_042

## Story ID

- ID Format: US_042

## Story Title

- No-Show Risk Tier Classification and Display

## Description

- As a **staff member**, I want each appointment's no-show risk score classified into Low, Medium, or High tiers displayed with combined color, icon, and text label indicators in queue and schedule views, so that I can quickly identify at-risk patients and prioritize outreach.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01: Risk score is classified into three tiers

- **Given**: An appointment has a calculated no-show risk score
- **When**: The classification algorithm runs
- **Then**: The score is classified into Low (green), Medium (amber), or High (red) tier based on configurable threshold boundaries, and the NoShowRiskTier field is stored on the Appointment record

### AC-02: Boundary scores are classified conservatively

- **Given**: A risk score falls exactly on the boundary between two tiers
- **When**: The classification is applied
- **Then**: The score is assigned to the higher-risk tier (conservative approach) — e.g., a score on the Low/Medium boundary is classified as Medium

### AC-03: Risk tier badge displays on same-day queue

- **Given**: The staff member views the same-day queue (SCR-017)
- **When**: Appointments with different risk tiers are displayed
- **Then**: Each appointment shows a risk tier badge with combined color (green/amber/red), icon (shield-check/alert-triangle/alert-octagon), and text label ("Low"/"Medium"/"High") per UXR-402

### AC-04: Risk tier badge displays on staff schedule view

- **Given**: The staff member views the staff schedule (SCR-022)
- **When**: Scheduled appointments are displayed
- **Then**: Each appointment slot shows the risk tier badge matching the same visual treatment as the queue view

### AC-05: Risk tier drives reminder escalation

- **Given**: An appointment is classified as Medium or High risk
- **When**: The reminder scheduling system processes the appointment
- **Then**: The appointment receives the escalated reminder sequence (7 days, 48 hours, 24 hours, and 2 hours) instead of the standard sequence

### AC-06: Risk tier is recalculated on reschedule

- **Given**: A patient reschedules an appointment
- **When**: The rescheduling is confirmed
- **Then**: The risk score and tier are recalculated with the updated lead time and time-of-day values

## Edge Cases

- **All appointments Low risk**: The queue and schedule views should still render correctly with all-green badges without performance impact
- **Risk tier display without color**: Per UXR-402, the tier must not be conveyed by color alone — the icon and text label must be sufficient for users with color vision deficiency
- **Configuration change recalculation**: If tier boundary thresholds are updated, existing appointment tiers should not be retroactively changed — only new calculations use the updated thresholds
