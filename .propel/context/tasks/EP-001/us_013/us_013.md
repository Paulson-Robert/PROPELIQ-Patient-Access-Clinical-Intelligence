# User Story - US_013

## Story ID

- ID Format: US_013

## Story Title

- Patient Registration and Social Login

## Description

- As a **patient**, I want to register and log in using my Google or Microsoft account or via email/password with email verification, so that I can securely access the platform using my preferred authentication method.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01: Google social login completes OAuth flow

- **Given**: The patient is on the login/registration page (SCR-001)
- **When**: The patient clicks "Continue with Google" and grants OAuth consent
- **Then**: The system receives the OAuth tokens, creates or links the patient account with the Patient role, issues a JWT session token with 15-minute sliding expiry, and redirects the patient to the patient dashboard (SCR-002)

### AC-02: Microsoft social login completes OAuth flow

- **Given**: The patient is on the login/registration page (SCR-001)
- **When**: The patient clicks "Continue with Microsoft" and grants OAuth consent
- **Then**: The system receives the OAuth tokens, creates or links the patient account with the Patient role, issues a JWT session token with 15-minute sliding expiry, and redirects the patient to the patient dashboard (SCR-002)

### AC-03: Email/password registration with email verification

- **Given**: The patient is on the registration form (SCR-001)
- **When**: The patient enters a valid email, creates a password meeting complexity requirements, and submits the form
- **Then**: The system creates the patient account with a "Pending Verification" status, sends a verification email with a one-time link, and displays a "Check your email" confirmation message

### AC-04: Email verification activates patient account

- **Given**: A patient has registered and received a verification email
- **When**: The patient clicks the verification link within the expiry period
- **Then**: The account status changes to "Active", the verification page (SCR-028) displays a success message, and the patient can log in

### AC-05: Social login denied consent shows alternative

- **Given**: The patient selects Google or Microsoft login
- **When**: The patient denies OAuth consent on the provider's screen
- **Then**: The system returns the patient to SCR-001 with an error message and presents the email/password registration option as an alternative

### AC-06: Duplicate email prevents registration

- **Given**: A patient account already exists with email "existing@example.com"
- **When**: A new registration is attempted with the same email
- **Then**: The system displays "An account with this email already exists" and offers login or password reset options

## Edge Cases

- **Expired verification link**: If the verification link expires (e.g., after 24 hours), the patient should see an "expired link" message with a "Resend verification" option
- **Social login email mismatch**: If a patient previously registered via email and later tries social login with the same email, the system should link the accounts rather than creating a duplicate
- **OAuth provider outage**: If Google or Microsoft OAuth services are unreachable, the login button should be disabled with a "Service temporarily unavailable" tooltip
